Russian Cinema — the optional extension

An optional extension to History of Russia.

An optional extension to the History of Russia, on the model of the side trip in an alumni travel brochure. The main course treats these films as EVIDENCE about the past; this one treats them as events in their own right. Films recurring from the main course are the point, not a duplication: Andrei Rublev is the fifteenth century there and Tarkovsky here; Potemkin is 1905 there and montage here.

English Literature on Film — the optional extension

An optional extension to History of England.

An optional extension to the History of England. The main course treats films as evidence about the past; this one follows the BOOKS. It exists because of a measurement: 585 films here are tagged "based on novel or book" and 570 of them are NOT on the Literature shelf. That shelf holds 75 items - Shakespeare and Dickens - because a shelf requires a physical move, which is why only two authors have one. Austen’s adaptations are scattered across SEVEN shelves; no shelf can hold her, but a syllabus can.

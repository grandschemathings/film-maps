Russian Literature on Film — the optional extension

An optional extension to History of Russia.

An optional extension to the History of Russia, and the shortest of these modules. The nineteenth-century Russian novel is arguably the most filmed body of literature outside English, and this collection holds sixteen of those films - against thirty-six for France. The gaps are the finding: Dostoevsky has ONE adaptation here, and Chekhov two. Where a session rests on a single film, that is the shelf speaking, not the subject.

# Film Maps

Two maps of a history film collection, placed by **where each film is set** — not where it
was shot and not where the studio was.

- **[Africa on Film](https://grandschemathings.github.io/film-maps/africa.html)** —
  170 films, 157 placements, 32 countries. Grey means no films yet; films tagged Africa but
  deliberately excluded are listed, so an omission is a decision on record.
- **[South American Cinema](https://grandschemathings.github.io/film-maps/south-america.html)** —
  every country from the 1530s conquest to the present, filterable by theme, and splittable
  between films made by South Americans and films made by outsiders.

Both pages load D3 and their country geometry from a CDN, so they need a network connection.

These are published copies. The catalogue they are built from is private; the maps are
regenerated there and copied here, so this repo is a destination rather than a source.

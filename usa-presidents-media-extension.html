An optional extension to The American Presidency. The main course asks who held the office; this one asks who told the country about it. ⭐ It exists because of a measurement: 36 films carry the hash tag #themedia and roughly fifteen of them are already presidency films — Frost/Nixon, Good Night and Good Luck, Wag the Dog, Mark Felt, The Ides of March — so the two subjects were already entangled in the library before anyone drew a syllabus across them. ⚠️ #themedia is one of seven surviving hash-prefixed tags and a search for the spaced form returns ZERO, so this extension is also an argument that it names a SUBJECT rather than a collection, and should be retired to a plain tag.

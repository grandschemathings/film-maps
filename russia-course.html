History of Russia — a film syllabus

A three-term chronological course in which the weekly work is a FILM rather than a book. Modelled on the Institute for the Study of Western Civilization syllabus, which pairs one spine text with a different supplementary work each week. Sessions with no film are the gaps in the collection, not in the history.

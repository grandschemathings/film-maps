Week 1

Roman Britain and the Leaving

Four centuries of province in one session: the invasion of AD 43, the Iceni revolt of 60, the frontier and its lost legion, and then the legions go and the record goes quiet. Two Boudicas sixteen years apart make the point that the revolt is told and retold; King Arthur (2004) is here rather than with the Arthur films because its claim is about the ROMANS leaving, not the myth. ⭐ The invasion seen from Rome’s end is in I, Claudius S01E11 “A God in Colchester” — Camulodunum, where Claudius was worshipped as a god — and S01E12, which credits Caractacus. ⚠️ Caesar’s raids of 55-54 BC - the FIRST Roman contact, and the real start of the story - are covered here by nothing but the two Asterix films, which are farce but are at least about the right invasion.

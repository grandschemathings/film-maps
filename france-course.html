History of France — a film syllabus

A three-term chronological course in which the weekly work is a FILM rather than a book. This one draws from the WHOLE library, not the France shelf: a national history course is inherently cross-shelf, and 50 of the films it needs live under Ancient/Rome, Wars/WW2, WW2-Holocaust, Africa and Wars/WW1. French cinema as a subject in its own right is the optional extension.

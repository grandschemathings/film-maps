The Invented Presidency — an extension

An optional extension to The American Presidency.

An optional extension to The American Presidency, on the office as imagined rather than recorded. ⭐ It exists because the `usa president` tag conflates the two: Dr. Strangelove’s Merkin Muffley sits in the same tag as Lincoln. For a course about the actual presidency that is a defect; as a course of its own it is the subject, because an invented president is where the office gets ARGUED ABOUT rather than commemorated. ⭐ The roster was built from CAST ROLES, not tags: a <role> naming a president whose surname is not a real president’s. About two dozen qualify, from Judson Hammond in 1933 to the unnamed president of Civil War in 2024.

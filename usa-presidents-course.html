The American Presidency — a film syllabus

A chronological course through the forty-five people who have held the office, in which the weekly work is a FILM. ⭐ Built from a QID-backed depiction index rather than a tag: the 45 presidents were taken from Wikidata P39, filtered to real humans, and matched against holdings through the five depiction routes in real-people-in-films.rq — cast role, main subject, depicts, cast-role-via-fictional-character, and recorded participant, UNIONED with a `President <name>` match on the cast roles. 35 of the 45 have at least one held depiction. ⚠️ Neither instrument alone is enough: the Wikidata routes reach Van Buren and Cleveland that the roles miss, and the roles reach Polk and Pierce that Wikidata misses. Two more, Hayes and Benjamin Harrison, are named in DIALOGUE and depicted by nobody, which leaves EIGHT presidents with nothing at all. ⚠️ Sessions with no film are gaps in the collection, not in the history, and the empty ones cluster so sharply that the shape is itself the lesson: American cinema has almost no interest in the presidents between Jackson and Lincoln, nor between Grant and Franklin Roosevelt.
